Banana Swing – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Banana Swing ("the game") is made by Mango Tide Games (Reagan Land). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

You can play without an account. We use Google's Firebase Analytics to learn how the game is played, so we can make it better, and you can switch this off in the game. If you choose to use Live Race, a small amount of extra information is kept on our server. There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your information.

How the game is played (Firebase Analytics)

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to send us information about how it's played, for example:

  • when the game is opened, how long it's played for, and which country it's played in (worked out roughly from your internet address, which isn't stored)
  • the kind of iPhone and the iOS version
  • game events such as starting a run, how far it went, finishing the tutorial, and opening the island or Live Race
  • in-app purchases (which item and its price, never your payment details)

Firebase gives each copy of the game a random ID so we can count players, but it isn't linked to your name, email, Apple ID or Live Race username, and it doesn't use your iPhone's advertising ID. We use this only to improve the game. Google processes this information for us and may do so outside the UK, including in the United States. Google keeps the detailed records for no more than 14 months. Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can switch this off at any time in the game: Settings → Share play stats.

Live Race: what we keep

If you create a username for Live Race, our server keeps:

  • your username and a random player number made for you
  • your friends list, friend requests and the players you have blocked
  • a scrambled copy of a secret key that lets your phone sign in as you
  • reports you make about another player, and reports other players make about you

We use this only to run races and friends lists, and to deal with rude names and abuse. Your friends can see your username and whether you're online. Other players can see your username in races.

During a race, your position and the animal and gear you're using are passed to the other players in that race as it happens. They aren't saved. There is no chat, only a fixed list of quick messages such as "Nice!". Please don't use your real name as your username.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, which handles this information for us and may process it outside the UK, including in the United States. To connect you, Cloudflare handles your internet (IP) address. We don't store it with your account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings → Delete account. This removes your username, friends, friend requests and blocks straight away. If someone reported you, or you reported someone, a record of that report may be kept so we can deal with abuse, but it no longer links to a report you made. We keep the rest for as long as you have an account.

Your game progress

Your progress (bananas, items, animals, your island, settings and so on) is saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, a backup copy of your save and your Live Race sign-in is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so your progress and purchases come back if you reinstall the game or get a new iPhone. This copy is stored by Apple in your account; we never see it. You can turn this off in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Purchases

In-app purchases and the Banana Club subscription are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy covers these purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

You can manage or cancel the Banana Club at any time in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Game Center

If you use Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards. This is controlled by Apple and your Game Center settings, not by us.

Children

You can play the whole single-player game without an account, and you can switch off play statistics in Settings. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before creating a Live Race username. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's account by emailing the address below.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law we use Live Race information to provide the feature you asked for and to keep players safe, and play statistics because we have a legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played so we can improve it. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed before the change goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: support@mangotidegames.com

If you email us, we only use your email address and message to reply to you.
