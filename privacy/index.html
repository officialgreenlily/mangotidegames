Banana Swing – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Banana Swing ("the game") is made by Mango Tide Games (Reagan Land). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

Banana Swing does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and you don't need an account to play.

Your game progress

Your progress (bananas, items, animals, your island, settings and so on) is saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, a backup copy of your save is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so your progress and purchases come back if you reinstall the game or get a new iPhone. This copy is stored by Apple in your account; we never see it. You can turn this off in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Purchases

In-app purchases and the Banana Club subscription are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy covers these purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

You can manage or cancel the Banana Club at any time in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Game Center

If you use Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards. This is controlled by Apple and your Game Center settings, not by us.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a feature is added that needs information), this page will be updated and the date at the top changed before the change goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: support@mangotidegames.com

If you email us, we only use your email address and message to reply to you.
