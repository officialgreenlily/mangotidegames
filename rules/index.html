Banana Swing – Race Rules

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Live Race lets you race real people using a username. These rules keep it friendly for everyone.

Usernames

  • No rude, hateful, sexual, threatening or bullying names.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else.
  • Don't use your real name, phone number or anything else that could identify you.

How we keep it safe

  • Rude words are filtered when you pick a name.
  • There's no chat in races, only a fixed list of quick messages.
  • You can report or block any player from your friends list or the starting line. Blocked players can't add or invite you.

If someone breaks the rules

We look at every report and aim to act within 24 hours. We have no tolerance for objectionable names or abusive behaviour: we can remove a name and ban a player from Live Race without warning. A ban doesn't affect the rest of your game or anything you've bought.

Get help

Report a player in the game, or email support@mangotidegames.com. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.

Your use of the game is also covered by Apple's standard terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/ and our Privacy Policy: https://mangotidegames.com/privacy/
