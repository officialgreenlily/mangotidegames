About the game

Banana Swing is a bright one-touch swinging game. Hold anywhere to grab a branch, let go to fly, and see how far you can go. Along the way you rescue monkey friends, dodge crocs and banana thieves, and ride everything from a bathtub to a submarine and a jet that blasts off into space.

Between runs, players walk around their own island as their favourite animal: growing a banana farm, hatching eggs, running a café, playing mini golf and sailing round the coast. With 60 levels, a Daily Challenge, several game modes and live races against friends, there's always something new to play.

Features

  • One-touch controls: hold to swing, release to fling
  • 26 animals to collect, each with its own special move, plus 24 pets
  • 24 rides, including a submarine dive and a trip to space
  • A walkable island with a farm, café, Fun Park and boat
  • 60 hand-made levels with bosses, and a new Daily Challenge every day
  • Live Race: add friends by username and race real people
  • Original psychedelic-pop soundtrack
  • Works offline, with no account needed to play
